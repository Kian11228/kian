The problem

Twenty pages, unread

Most published research is only ever skimmed — its evidence locked in static figures and dense prose.

The format

Papers as investigations

Readers predict outcomes, run the experiments, and watch the evidence unfold — reaching the conclusion before being told it.

The rule

The paper is the truth

Every number, figure, and definition comes directly from the published work. Nothing invented, ever.

How a paper becomes an experience.

A repeatable format — hook, explore, discover, validate — built with the authors, faithful to the publication.

01 · Hook

Start with the surprise

Every paper has one moment that made its authors sit up. The experience opens there — not with the abstract.

02 · Explore

Reader runs the evidence

Hypotheses before results. Sliders, toggles, and live experiments instead of static figures — the reader investigates.

03 · Validate

Anchored to the publication

Every interactive view pairs with the exact published figure. One toggle between experience and paper, always.
