Why the format exists

The problem

Twenty pages, unread

Most published research is only ever skimmed, its evidence locked in static figures and dense prose.

The format

Papers as investigations

Readers predict outcomes, run the experiments, and watch the evidence unfold, reaching the conclusion before being told it.

The rule

The paper is the truth

Every number, figure, and definition comes directly from the source manuscript. Nothing invented, ever.

How a manuscript becomes auditable.

A repeatable format (hook, explore, discover, validate) built with the authors and faithful to the manuscript.

01 · Hook

Start with the surprise

Every paper has one moment that made its authors sit up. The article opens there, not with the abstract.

02 · Explore

Reader runs the evidence

Hypotheses before results. Sliders, toggles, and live experiments instead of static figures: the reader investigates.

03 · Validate

Anchored to the publication

Every interactive view pairs with the exact manuscript figure. One toggle between article and manuscript, always.

Built by a researcher, for researchers.
